================================================================================
1. PROJECT OVERVIEW & PROBLEM STATEMENT
================================================================================

1.1 BACKGROUND & PROBLEM STATEMENT:
Traditional dairy farming operations suffer from significant inefficiencies:
- Manual Paper Ledgers: Milk yield recordings, vaccination histories, and feed
  stocks are written on paper, leading to lost records, errors, and delayed insights.
- Individual Cattle Identification: Ear tag numbers are difficult to read in barns
  and prone to misidentification during daily milking and medical treatment.
- Delayed Health Interventions: Missed vaccine boosters (FMD, Brucellosis, Blackleg)
  cause sudden disease outbreaks and massive herd mortality or milk yield crashes.
- Unmonitored Feed Waste: Feed constitutes 60-70% of dairy farm operational costs.
  Without real-time inventory and consumption logging, overfeeding or stockouts occur.
- Lack of Financial Transparency: Farm owners struggle to calculate daily profit
  margins because milk revenue and operational expenditures (feed, medicines, labor,
  electricity) are tracked in separate, uncoordinated books.

1.2 THE SOLUTION - SMART DAIRY FARM MANAGEMENT SYSTEM:
A centralized, mobile-responsive web portal designed specifically for modern dairy
enterprises and academic evaluation. The system digitizes every facet of farm life:
1. Generates unique, high-resolution QR tags for every cattle.
2. Enables real-time camera scanning via phone or webcam for instant cattle profiles.
3. Provides high-speed batch milking session logging (Morning/Evening/Afternoon).
4. Tracks nutritional feed inventories with low-stock warning triggers.
5. Manages automated vaccination reminders with color-coded alerts.
6. Maintains staff rosters and categorized operational expense ledgers.
7. Displays interactive visual financial and production analytics with Chart.js.
8. Enforces a strict 3-tier Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) security model.

2.2 KEY ARCHITECTURAL CHARACTERISTICS:
- Modular Blueprint Structure: Instead of a monolithic app file, features are
  separated into clean functional blueprints (`auth`, `main`, `animal`, `milk`,
  `feed`, `vaccination`, `employee`, `expense`, `report`, `qr`).
- Dual Database Engine Support:
  * Zero-Configuration Local Development: Defaults to SQLite (`dairy.db`), requiring
    no background database service installation.
  * Enterprise Production Support: Fully compatible with MySQL via PyMySQL connector
    and SQLAlchemy connection string pooling (`mysql+pymysql://...`).
  * Auto-Seeding: Automatically provisions tables and realistic sample records upon
    first boot if the database is blank.
- Network-Aware Context Injection:
  * Automatically resolves local LAN IP address via UDP socket probe (`8.8.8.8:80`).
  * Injects `local_ip` and `mobile_url` into all Jinja2 templates via context
    processor, allowing farm staff to connect their smartphones instantly.
- Stateless REST & State-Bearing SSR Hybrid:
  * Server-Side Rendered (SSR) HTML with Jinja2 for fast page loads and rich layout.
  * REST JSON APIs (`/api/qr/lookup/<tag>`, `/api/animals/search`) for asynchronous
    live camera QR scanning and search autocomplete without reloading the page.

3. 3-TIER ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL (RBAC) & SECURITY MODEL
================================================================================

To guarantee farm data integrity and prevent unauthorized record tampering, the
system replaces public sign-ups with a strict Administrator-Governed RBAC model.

3.1 THE THREE USER ROLES:
1. Administrator (`admin`):
   - Scope: Farm Owner, Managing Director, System Administrator.
   - Permissions: Full unrestricted control. The ONLY role capable of creating user
     logins, editing credentials, resetting passwords, toggling active status, and
     viewing sensitive financial ledgers and system logs.
2. Farm Manager (`manager`):
   - Scope: Barn Supervisor, Lead Herd Specialist, Chief Veterinarian.
   - Permissions: Herd lifecycle management (adding/editing cattle, generating QR tags),
     feed restocking, setting up vaccination schedules, logging batch milk entries,
     managing staff records, logging operational expenses, and viewing analytics.
3. Farm Staff (`staff`):
   - Scope: Daily Milkers, Barn Hands, Feed Handlers.
   - Permissions: Routine barn operations only: logging daily milk yields, scanning
     cattle QR tags with phones, recording feed rations used, and marking vaccinations
     as completed. Restricted from viewing user accounts, salary data, or financial
     analytics.

3.2 RBAC PERMISSION MATRIX:
+-------------------------------+---------------+---------------+---------------+
| Feature / Action              | Admin         | Manager       | Staff         |
+-------------------------------+---------------+---------------+---------------+
| Create/Manage User Logins     | FULL ACCESS   | BLOCKED       | BLOCKED       |
| Add / Edit Cattle Master Data | FULL ACCESS   | FULL ACCESS   | VIEW ONLY     |
| Generate / Download QR Tags   | FULL ACCESS   | FULL ACCESS   | VIEW / SCAN   |
| Real-time Camera QR Scanner   | FULL ACCESS   | FULL ACCESS   | FULL ACCESS   |
| Daily Milk Yield Entry        | FULL ACCESS   | FULL ACCESS   | DAILY ENTRY   |
| Session Batch Milk Entry      | FULL ACCESS   | FULL ACCESS   | DAILY ENTRY   |
| Feed Inventory & Restocking   | FULL ACCESS   | FULL ACCESS   | VIEW STOCK    |
| Log Daily Feed Consumption    | FULL ACCESS   | FULL ACCESS   | FULL ACCESS   |
| Vaccination Schedule & Edit   | FULL ACCESS   | FULL ACCESS   | MARK COMPLETE |
| Staff Directory & Payroll     | FULL ACCESS   | MANAGE STAFF  | BLOCKED       |
| Operational Expense Ledger    | FULL ACCESS   | LOG EXPENSES  | BLOCKED       |
| Financial & Production Reports| FULL ACCESS   | FULL ACCESS   | BLOCKED       |
+-------------------------------+---------------+---------------+---------------+

3.3 SECURITY SAFEGUARDS:
- Password Hashing: Implements Werkzeug's `generate_password_hash` utilizing
  salted hashing algorithms (PBKDF2/scrypt). Plaintext passwords are never stored.
- Session Protection: Flask-Login session management with `login_required` and
  custom `@admin_required` decorators preventing URL parameter tampering.
- Active Flag Enforcement: If an administrator deactivates a user account, their
  active session is invalidated and login attempts are blocked immediately.
- CSRF & SQL Injection Immunity: Parameterized SQL queries via SQLAlchemy ORM;
  safe string sanitization across form inputs.

4. CORE MODULES & FUNCTIONAL WORKFLOWS
================================================================================

4.1 CATTLE MASTER REGISTER & DIGITAL PEDIGREE:
- Purpose: Maintains the complete historical and biological dossier for every cow,
  buffalo, or goat in the herd.
- Data Tracked: Tag number (unique ear tag), name, species, breed (e.g., Holstein
  Friesian, Jersey, Gir, Sahiwal, Murrah Buffalo), gender, date of birth, weight,
  milking status (`Milking`, `Dry`, `Pregnant`, `Sick`, `Calf`, `Sold`), and
  health status (`Healthy`, `Under Treatment`, `Quarantined`, `Critical`).
- Dynamic Computed Properties:
  * `age`: Calculates years and months from date of birth dynamically.
  * `today_milk`: Sums milk recorded for today's date across all milking sessions.
  * `total_milk`: Aggregates lifetime milk yield produced by this individual animal.
- Actions: Add cattle, update status, view detailed dossier, filter by milking
  or health status with one-click filter pills.

4.2 DIGITAL QR CODE TAGGING & REAL-TIME CAMERA SCANNER:
- QR Generation (`utils/qr_helper.py`):
  * Utilizes `qrcode` and `Pillow (PIL)` graphics library.
  * Encodes unique cattle tag number into high-error-correction (Level H) QR matrix.
  * Automatically embeds a contrasting branded tag banner at the base of the image
    displaying `TAG: DF-101` for human visual readability.
  * Generates high-resolution PNGs saved in `static/qr_codes/`.
- Printable QR Ear Tags:
  * Administrators and managers can download individual QR badges (`/qr/download/<id>`)
    or print high-contrast sheets for laminating and attaching to cattle collars/ear tags.
- Live In-Browser Camera Scanner (`routes/qr.py` & `templates/scan_qr.html`):
  * Employs `html5-qrcode` JavaScript engine for hardware camera access (webcam or
    rear mobile phone camera).
  * Automatically scans frame-by-frame for QR codes.
  * Upon detection, sounds an audio confirmation beep and executes an asynchronous
    fetch request to `/api/qr/lookup/<tag_number>`.
  * Instantly displays an interactive modal card with cattle photo/tag, age, breed,
    current milking status, health condition, last milk yield, and upcoming vaccines.
  * Includes manual tag search fallback with autocomplete search input.

4.3 DAILY MILK YIELD MONITORING & SESSION BATCH ENTRY:
- Purpose: Tracks the core revenue driver of the dairy enterprise.
- Milking Sessions: Morning, Afternoon, and Evening.
- Quality & Financial Fields:
  * `quantity_liters`: Milk volume logged.
  * `fat_percentage`: Fat content percentage (default 4.0%).
  * `snf_percentage`: Solids-Not-Fat percentage (default 8.5%).
  * `rate_per_liter`: Calculated or market payout rate in INR (₹) per liter.
  * `total_amount`: Stored calculated field = `quantity_liters * rate_per_liter`.
- Two Milking Entry Modes:
  1. Single Record Entry: Detailed logging for individual cattle with remarks and fat/SNF.
  2. Session Batch Entry Grid (`/milk/quick-entry`):
     * Solves the real-world farm challenge where 20-50 cows are milked in a single hour.
     * Displays all active milking cattle in an Excel-like interactive entry table.
     * Barn staff enter morning or evening liters in rapid succession and click
       "Save Batch Records" once to bulk-insert all records in a single transaction.
- Filtering & Pagination:
  * Filter by Date Range, Milking Session, and individual Animal ID.
  * Displays real-time summary statistics cards for the filtered dataset: Total Milk
    Yield, Total Revenue (₹), Morning Milk, Evening Milk, and Average Fat %.
  * Clean pagination (5 records per page) with page jump controls.

4.4 FEED & RATION INVENTORY MANAGEMENT:
- Purpose: Prevents malnutrition, overfeeding, and unexpected feed stockouts.
- Feed Classification:
  * Green Fodder (Napier grass, Maize fodder, Berseem)
  * Dry Fodder (Wheat straw, Paddy straw)
  * Concentrates (Cattle feed pellets, Mustard cake, Cottonseed cake)
  * Mineral Mixture & Vitamins
  * Silage (Fermented green maize silage)
- Key Metrics:
  * `current_stock` & `unit` (e.g., kg, quintals).
  * `unit_cost` & `stock_value` (monetary value of capital tied up in feed).
  * `minimum_threshold`: Low stock alert threshold.
- Stock Warning Engine:
  * Color-coded status badges (`Adequate` in green, `Low Stock Alert` in pulsing red).
  * Dashboard alert counters warning managers when critical rations run low.
- Daily Consumption Logger (`FeedConsumption`):
  * When staff feed animals, they record the quantity used and recipient group.
  * Automatically calculates cost of feed consumed and deducts the quantity from
    the master inventory in a single atomic database operation.

4.5 CATTLE HEALTH & IMMUNIZATION SCHEDULING:
- Purpose: Ensures zero missed vaccine boosters and comprehensive veterinary tracking.
- Pre-Configured Vaccine Schedules:
  * Foot and Mouth Disease (FMD) - Biannual
  * Brucellosis (S19) - Heifer immunization
  * Blackleg (Clostridial) - Annual
  * Haemorrhagic Septicaemia (HS) - Pre-monsoon
  * Anthrax spore vaccine
  * Periodic Deworming cycles
- Intelligent Dynamic Status Engine:
  * Compares `next_due_date` against `date.today()`.
  * `Overdue`: Red alert badge if the due date has passed and status is not Completed.
  * `Upcoming`: Amber alert badge if due within the next 30 days.
  * `Completed`: Green badge for successfully administered immunizations.
- One-Click Advance Action:
  * Staff or managers click "Administer Booster" to update records and automatically
    schedule the next due date based on veterinary protocols.

4.6 FARM WORKFORCE DIRECTORY & PAYROLL:
- Purpose: Tracks human capital, daily duties, and monthly labor expenditures.
- Staff Information: Name, role (e.g., Head Milker, Animal Caretaker, Visiting
  Veterinarian, Store In-charge), phone number, email, monthly salary (₹), joining
  date, employment status (`Active`, `On Leave`, `Inactive`), and residential address.
- Financial Aggregation:
  * Calculates total monthly payroll commitment for financial reports.

4.7 OPERATIONAL EXPENSE LEDGER & COST ACCOUNTING:
- Purpose: Gives farm management 100% visibility into daily operational expenditures.
- Expense Categories:
  1. Feed & Fodder Purchases
  2. Veterinary & Medical Care
  3. Maintenance & Equipment Repairs (milking machines, chillers, generators)
  4. Labor & Wage Payouts
  5. Utilities & Electricity (cooling tanks, water pumps, lighting)
  6. Equipment & Machinery
  7. Fuel & Transportation
  8. Miscellaneous & Contingencies
- Transaction Fields: Title, category, amount (₹), payment date, payee/vendor name,
  payment method (`Cash`, `UPI`, `Bank Transfer`, `Cheque`), invoice/receipt number,
  and descriptive notes.

4.8 EXECUTIVE ANALYTICS & MANAGEMENT REPORTS:
- Purpose: Delivers visual, decision-ready intelligence to farm owners and evaluators.
- Interactive Chart.js Visualizations:
  1. 7-Day Milk Production Curve: Compares Morning vs Evening production trends.
  2. Herd Status & Health Breakdown: Doughnut chart visualizing Milking, Dry,
     Pregnant, and Sick proportions.
  3. Financial Performance Comparison: Daily milk sales revenue vs operational
     expenses over time.
- Date-Range Filterable Analytics:
  * Set arbitrary date ranges (default: last 30 days).
  * Computes Total Milk Yield, Total Milk Revenue, Average Daily Milk, Total Expenses,
    and Net Operating Profit / Loss.
- Top Milkers Leaderboard:
  * Identifies the top 5 highest-producing cattle in the herd with rank badges.
- Print-Ready Format:
  * Built-in CSS print media styles allowing 1-click printing or PDF export for
    monthly farm audits and project presentations.

4.9 VETERINARY MEDICAL TREATMENTS & MILK WITHHOLDING TRACKING:
- Direct Clinical Logging: Records cattle diagnoses, clinical symptoms, administered
  medications (antibiotics, anti-inflammatories), attending veterinarian, and dosage dates.
- Food Safety Milk Withdrawal Safeguard: Enforces mandatory milk withdrawal days
  (e.g., 3-7 days during antibiotic courses). Cattle under active withdrawal are flagged
  with prominent badges on the Milk Production register, Cattle dossier, and Treatment logs
  to prevent antibiotic-contaminated milk from entering bulk distribution.
- Treatment Status Lifecycle: Tracks cases through `Active Treatment`, `Observation`,
  and `Completed / Cured`, recording recovery dates and clinical outcomes.

4.10 ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE & PREDICTIVE ANALYTICS WEB HUB (`/ai-insights`):
- A unified 6-tab intelligence command center featuring real-time interactive models:
  1. 14-Day Milk Forecasting (Herd & individual cows with 95% confidence bands).
  2. Early Illness Anomaly Alerts (Rolling z-scores flagging >=20% yield crashes).
  3. Mastitis Risk Predictor (0-100% composite risk meter with clinical action checklists).
  4. Cull & Keep Optimizer (K-Means profitability clustering into 3 management tiers).
  5. Precision Feed Ration Optimization (SciPy linear programming least-cost ICAR/NRC rations).
  6. Financial Forecasting (30-day projection of operating expenses, revenue, and net margin).

6. APPLICATION EXECUTION FLOW (FROM BOOT TO RENDER)
================================================================================

Step 1: Invocation
  - Command: `python app.py`
  - The root wrapper script ensures python path resolution and triggers the
    application factory located in `DairyFarmQR-main/app.py`.

Step 2: Configuration & Directory Setup (`config.py`)
  - Detects if `USE_MYSQL` is active. If true, builds MySQL connection string;
    otherwise falls back to SQLite (`dairy.db`).
  - Ensures static folders exist: `static/images/` and `static/qr_codes/`.

Step 3: App Factory & Extension Initialization (`create_app`)
  - Flask instance is initialized with secret keys and configuration.
  - SQLAlchemy (`db.init_app`) binds the database.
  - Flask-Login (`login_manager.init_app`) sets the default login view (`auth.login`).
  - Registers 10 modular blueprints (`auth`, `main`, `animal`, `milk`, `feed`,
    `vaccination`, `employee`, `expense`, `report`, `qr`).

Step 4: Auto-Database Creation & Demo Data Seeding
  - Inside `with app.app_context():`, invokes `db.create_all()` to ensure all
    tables exist.
  - Runs `seed_initial_data(app)`:
    * Provisions 3 default accounts: `admin` (admin123), `manager` (manager123),
      `staff` (staff123).
    * Provisions 5 high-pedigree cattle (DF-101 to DF-105) and generates their QR codes.
    * Seeds 7-day milk production records across morning and evening sessions.
    * Seeds feed inventory items and recent ration consumption.
    * Seeds vaccination timelines (demonstrating Overdue, Upcoming, Completed).
    * Seeds employees and realistic farm operational expense records.

Step 5: Network Address Resolution & Server Launch
  - Probes local IP address via UDP socket.
  - Injects `local_ip` and `mobile_url` into the global template context.
  - Starts development server on `0.0.0.0:5000`, making the system simultaneously
    accessible via desktop browser (`http://localhost:5000`) and farm staff
    smartphones on the same Wi-Fi (`http://<LAN-IP>:5000`).

Step 6: User Request Lifecycle
  - User visits `/dashboard`.
  - Flask-Login checks session cookie. If not authenticated, redirects to `/login`.
  - If authenticated, queries active herd stats, milk yields, feed stock alerts,
    and vaccine schedules.
  - Renders `dashboard.html` extending `layout.html` with role-aware sidebar links.

10. KEY HIGHLIGHTS & VIVA / PRESENTATION DEFENSE CHEATSHEET
================================================================================

When explaining or presenting this project for an evaluation, viva, or review,
highlight these 6 architectural differentiators:

1. Real-World Digital Tagging (Not Just a Generic CRUD App):
   Instead of typing long alphanumeric IDs in dusty barn conditions, staff use
   their smartphone camera to scan physical QR ear tags. The `html5-qrcode` engine
   decodes the tag in real-time and fetches the animal's lifetime yield, health
   condition, and vaccine history via AJAX in under 200 milliseconds.

2. Session-Based Batch Milking Grid:
   Traditional database forms require opening and submitting a form 30 separate
   times for 30 cows. This system introduces a spreadsheet-style batch entry grid
   where all active cows are listed simultaneously, saving 90% of data entry time.

3. 3-Tier Enterprise RBAC with Disabled Public Sign-Ups:
   Many student projects make the mistake of leaving public registration open.
   In an actual commercial farm, an unauthorized user should never be able to create
   an account. This system locks registration to authenticated administrators only.

4. Intelligent Stock & Vaccine Engines:
   - Feed: Calculates consumption costs and automatically subtracts quantities from
     warehouse inventories while throwing low-stock alerts.
   - Health: Compares booster dates against `date.today()` dynamically to categorize
     immunizations into Overdue (urgent), Upcoming, or Completed.

5. Dual-Database Fallback Pattern:
   Designed to work out-of-the-box on any examiner's machine via SQLite without
   demanding MySQL installation, yet fully equipped with enterprise MySQL schema DDL
   and connection pooling for enterprise production deployment.

6. Comprehensive Financial Cost-Revenue Modeling:
   Correlates daily milk production revenue against 8 categories of operational
   expenses (feed, veterinary, electricity, labor, maintenance) to compute actual
   net farm profitability over any custom date range.

11. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE & MACHINE LEARNING SUBSYSTEM (6 CORE MODULES)
================================================================================

The system includes a production-grade AI & Predictive Analytics engine
located at `services/ai_engine.py` with dedicated UI routes at `/ai-insights`
and REST API endpoints at `/api/ai/*`:

11.1 MILK YIELD FORECASTING (HERD & INDIVIDUAL CATTLE):
- Objective: Predicts milk yield for the next 7 to 14 days for each individual
  cow and across the entire farm herd.
- Algorithmic Tools: scikit-learn `RandomForestRegressor` and `GradientBoostingRegressor`
  with autoregressive lag features (lag 1, lag 2, lag 7, rolling 7-day average, session split).
- Inputs: Historical milk production logs, days in milk (lactation stage), session
  (Morning/Evening), breed, animal live weight, and age.
- Output: An interactive Chart.js line on the Reports (`/reports`) and AI Hub
  (`/ai-insights`) pages displaying future projected liters, session splits, and 95%
  confidence interval bands (expanding uncertainty envelope).

11.2 ANOMALY DETECTION (EARLY ILLNESS & STRESS WARNING):
- Objective: Detects sudden, sharp drops in milk yield or abnormal fat/SNF fluctuations
  that indicate early-stage mastitis, heat stress, ketosis, or acute illness.
- Algorithmic Tools: Dual-engine architecture combining rolling 7-day moving averages
  with Z-scores ($Z \le -1.8$) and scikit-learn `IsolationForest` multi-dimensional outlier scoring.
- Output: Immediate alert banners across the Dashboard, Milk Production Register, and
  Cattle Profile:
  "DF-102 gave 38% less than her 7-day average. Check her health."
  Includes specific clinical directives (CMT test, rectal temperature check, quarter isolation).

11.3 MASTITIS RISK PREDICTION RADAR:
- Objective: Computes a multi-factor risk score (0% to 100%) to identify subclinical and
  clinical mastitis before permanent udder damage occurs.
- Algorithmic Tools: Calibrated Logistic Regression & Random Forest Risk Classifier.
- Integrated Treatment Log Module: Connects directly with `MedicalTreatment` records to
  factor in past mastitis recurrence history (last 90 days) and active antibiotic treatments.
- Inputs: Yield drop percentage, Solids-Not-Fat (SNF) deficit below the 8.5% epithelial
  integrity threshold, fat instability, days in milk, and prior clinical treatments.
- Output: Risk categorization (High Risk >=70%, Moderate Risk 40-69%, Low Risk <40%),
  contributing factor diagnostic lists, and veterinary intervention advice.

11.4 CULL AND KEEP RECOMMENDATION MATRIX:
- Objective: Evaluates herd efficiency and clusters cows into economic profitability tiers.
- Algorithmic Tools: scikit-learn `KMeans` clustering ($k=3$) and Composite Economic
  Contribution Index ($Score = Revenue_{milk} - Cost_{feed} - Cost_{vet} - Penalty_{age}$).
- Tiers & Output:
  - High Profit (Keep & Breed): Elite yielders with superior feed conversion efficiency.
  - Average Profit (Maintain & Monitor): Consistent yielders covering operational margins.
  - Low Profit (Cull Candidate): Cows with negative contribution margins where veterinary
    or feed expenses exceed milk value.

11.5 FEED OPTIMIZATION & INVENTORY STOCKOUT FORECASTING:
- Objective: Formulates the cheapest daily ration meeting 100% nutritional requirements
  and forecasts warehouse feed stockout depletion dates.
- Algorithmic Tools:
  - Ration Optimization: `scipy.optimize.linprog` (Linear Programming) solving least-cost
    formulation subject to ICAR/NRC nutritional constraints (Dry Matter, Crude Protein CP,
    and Total Digestible Nutrients TDN based on cattle weight and daily milk yield).
  - Depletion Forecasting: Time-series consumption burn rates ($kg/day$) against warehouse
    stocks, predicting exact runout dates and automated 30-day buffer reorder quantities.
- Output: Daily feed ration breakdown (kg and ₹ cost per cow/day) and urgency countdowns
  (e.g., "Wheat Straw: Runs out in 13 days - Reorder by 3 Nov").

11.6 EXPENSE AND PROFIT FORECASTING (NEXT MONTH FINANCIAL HORIZON):
- Objective: Forecasts next month's (30 days) operational costs, milk revenues, and net farm margin.
- Algorithmic Tools: Time-series autoregressive linear trend projection with weekly seasonality
  factors and category weighting across feed, labor, utilities, veterinary, and maintenance.
- Output: 30-day trajectory chart on Reports and AI Hub, projected gross revenue (₹),
  projected operating costs (₹), forecasted net profit (₹), and expected net margin percentage.

11.7 VETERINARY TREATMENT & MILK WITHDRAWAL TRACKING:
- Database Model: `MedicalTreatment` table tracking animal ID, clinical diagnosis, symptoms,
  medications given, attending vet, and milk withdrawal period (withholding days).
- Food Safety: Automatically flags cattle whose milk is currently in withdrawal period,
  preventing contaminated milk from entering the bulk farm sales tank.
